Before Christian

FILE 001 • MEMORY LOADING

Before I met Christian, everything in my life felt normal. I was so naïve back then. I never understood why some people disliked me for no reason, and honestly, I still didn't understand it.

Every time I came home from school, I would overthink everything. Eventually, it became too much. Since I was bored, I decided to do some housework instead.

I cleaned the bedrooms, washed the dishes, organized the living room, folded all the clothes perfectly, and did the laundry.

It was pretty normal—except for the music playing beside me the entire time. The mood was sad, and I didn't even know why, but I kept going anyway.

✦ ✧ ✦

When I finally finished all the chores, I jumped onto my bed and stared at the ceiling. I let out the deepest sigh.

“What if something unexpected happens?”

Then I turned my head to the left and saw my phone lying there, still playing the song “Enough.”

I reached for it, turned up the volume, and flopped back onto my bed. I started scrolling through TikTok until I saw a video about chatting with friends online.

I was bored, so I opened the Play Store and searched for Litmatch.
