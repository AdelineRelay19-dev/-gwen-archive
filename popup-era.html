The Pop Up Era

Instead of downloading Litmatch, I saw an app called Pop up. It looked cuter and honestly, very me 😎.

Except… it took forever. The little circle just kept going round and round while the percentage crawled from 10% to 11% to 12%. Then I remembered: my storage was full. 💀

I kept staring at the screen, waiting for it to finish. At some point, I must’ve fallen asleep—because when I woke up, it was already morning.

By the time I remembered I’d downloaded Pop Up, it was almost evening. I opened it, signed in, picked an avatar, and found a bunch of games on the homepage. Of course, I had to try them. They were actually fun.

Then I saw the button: "Find your match". One click... and that’s how I met this idiot that, up until now, I honestly can't believe I'm still stuck with. 🙄✨
