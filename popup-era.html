The Pop Up Era

Instead of downloading Litmatch, I saw an app called Pop up. It looked cuter and honestly, very me 😎.

So I downloaded it... except it kept circling forever: 10%, 11%, 12%... tapos bigla kong naalala na full storage pala ako 💀. Nakatitig lang ako hanggang sa makatulog ako, paggising ko umaga na agad!

Almost evening na nung naalala kong buksan. Classic setup: sign in, create avatar, tapos may mini games pa so naaliw muna ako.

Then I saw the button: "Find your match". One click... and that’s how I met this idiot that, up until now, I honestly can't believe I'm still stuck with. 🙄✨
